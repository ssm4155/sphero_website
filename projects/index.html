Projects that close an evidence gap.

Not generic portfolio assignments. Each one is chosen because your target role needs proof you do not yet have.

From brief to updated readiness.

  1. 01
    Brief

    Why this project matters for your target, and which capabilities it exercises.

  2. 02
    Constraints

    Realistic data, tool and time limits, with optional stretch goals.

  3. 03
    Build

    You work independently, with bounded AI assistance.

  4. 04
    Review

    Rubric-based review with evidence, not only a generated summary.

  5. 05
    Portfolio

    An optional polished artifact or case study you keep.

  6. 06
    State update

    The result changes your readiness and what SPHERO recommends next.

A sample brief.

Review an experiment that a team shipped too early

Closes the gap: experiment design and communication. Given a dataset and a short write-up, you decide whether the launch was justified and write the recommendation.

  • Rubric: correctness of the analysis
  • Rubric: handling of multiple metrics and interference
  • Rubric: clarity of the recommendation for a non-technical reader
