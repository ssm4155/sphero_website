Billing questions.

What is metered?

Plans will state limits in units you can see, such as mock interviews per month. There will be no token accounting or credit matrix to decode.

Can I cancel?

Cancellation and renewal terms will sit next to the button, before you pay.

Is there a refund policy?

Billing FAQ and refund terms are published with pricing. Nothing here is a guarantee of a job or an interview.

Is there a team or employer plan?

Not yet. Sphero is built for candidates first.
