Five kinds of gap, kept apart.

A conceptual gap is not an interview format, and a missing tool is not missing experience.

  • Knowledge

    You cannot yet explain or reason about the concept.

  • Applied skill

    You know it, but cannot yet do it under real conditions.

  • Interview format

    A round type you have not practiced. Coding counts only when the target process includes coding.

  • Experience evidence

    The role expects proof of scope that your resume does not show.

  • Tooling and domain

    Specific tools or domain knowledge a company expects.

Inferred is not the same as confirmed.

Every gap carries a state: needs grounding, likely gap, or covered on your resume. SPHERO does not pretend an inference is a fact.
